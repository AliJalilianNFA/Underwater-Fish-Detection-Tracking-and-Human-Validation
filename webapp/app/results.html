Understanding the Results Dashboard

Candidate: a possible object linked across sampled video frames, not a confirmed fish.

Strong evidence: maximum track confidence at least 0.30. A high confidence score does not prove an animal is present.

In count window: the track was observed in the central, provisional one-second window.

FISH: a reviewer classified the candidate as fish. Multiple tracks may refer to the same animal, so this is not abundance.

Review progress: the percentage of candidates with a saved, non-empty review decision.

Validation: precision/recall require independently annotated frames. They are never calculated from confidence scores alone.
