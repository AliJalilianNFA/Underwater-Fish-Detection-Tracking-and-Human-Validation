Help — How to read ClipSift results

Each subsample has a center image taken from the original video. AI detections are extracted from sampled frames in the surrounding context, not necessarily the center image.

Context window: usually ±5 seconds around the sample. Count window: provisional central 1 second. Candidate track: a linked sequence of possible detections, not a fish count.

Strong evidence: maximum track confidence ≥0.30. Additional evidence: below 0.30. Neither means confirmed fish. HIGH / REVIEW: separate V6 trajectory priorities.

What to look for: visible fish shape and motion over adjacent frames; fixed pipes, cables and background; multiple tracks pointing to the same animal; objects inside the count window. Check the original footage when uncertain.

Review decisions: FISH, INVERTEBRATE, EQUIPMENT, BACKGROUND, or UNCERTAIN. Never convert candidate count directly into fish abundance.
